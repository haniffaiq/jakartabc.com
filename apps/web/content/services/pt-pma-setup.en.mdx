<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

A PT Penanaman Modal Asing (PT PMA) is the standard limited-liability vehicle for foreign-owned business in Indonesia. Minimum paid-up capital is IDR 2.5M under BKPM Reg 5/2025. Typical setup takes 4–6 weeks once documents are complete.

<h2 id="who-for">Who it's for</h2>

- Foreign investors entering Indonesia for the first time
- Existing foreign businesses formalizing a local entity
- Foreign founders relocating to operate from Jakarta

<h2 id="requirements">Requirements</h2>

- Passport scan for all foreign shareholders and directors
- Address proof from any country
- Proposed business activity so we can map KBLI codes
- Statement of investment plan
- Local-address option if needed

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>

<strong>Can I be the sole shareholder?</strong>

Yes, PT PMA permits 100% foreign ownership in most sectors, subject to the Positive Investment List.

<strong>Do I need to be in Jakarta during setup?</strong>

Not for most steps. A few sector permits may require in-person presence.
