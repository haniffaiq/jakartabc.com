<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

Some Indonesian activities need more than a company deed and OSS registration. We map your business model to KBLI codes, identify risk level, and prepare the sector permit path before you commit to a launch date.

<h2 id="who-for">Who it's for</h2>

- PT PMA founders unsure which KBLI codes fit the product
- Companies adding a regulated line of business after setup
- Investors comparing several sectors before choosing an entry route

<h2 id="requirements">Requirements</h2>

- Short description of the activity, customer type, and revenue model
- Current NIB or draft PT PMA documents if already available
- Proposed location, warehouse, clinic, office, or online-only footprint
- Any supplier, franchise, import, or professional-license documents
- Target go-live date so we can flag timing risk early

<h2 id="timeline">Timeline</h2>

Timing depends on the ministry and whether field inspection is required. Indicative milestones:

- Week 1 — KBLI and risk-level assessment
- Week 2 — OSS data alignment and application package
- Week 3–4 — Sector submission and clarification responses
- Week 5–8 — Technical recommendation, inspection, or permit issuance when required

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>

<strong>Can you guarantee a permit outcome?</strong>

No. We can make the application complete, factual, and defensible. Final approval remains with the relevant authority.

<strong>Is this needed before PT PMA setup?</strong>

Often yes. The correct KBLI set affects company deed language, OSS registration, and whether foreign ownership is allowed.

<strong>What government fee should I budget?</strong>

Many OSS updates have no fixed government fee. Regulated sectors may add ministry, inspection, or professional-license costs; we confirm those in the written scope.
