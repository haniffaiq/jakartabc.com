<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

Investor KITAS gives eligible foreign shareholders or directors a legal stay basis while they build and supervise an Indonesian company. We align the immigration file with PT PMA documents so ownership, role, and sponsor data are consistent.

<h2 id="who-for">Who it's for</h2>

- Foreign shareholders who need to spend extended time in Indonesia
- Directors supervising setup, hiring, banking, or regulated operations
- Families planning residency after the principal investor route is confirmed

<h2 id="requirements">Requirements</h2>

- Passport scan with sufficient validity and blank pages
- PT PMA deed, Kemenkumham approval, NIB, and NPWP
- Shareholding and director appointment evidence
- Recent photo and domicile or stay-address details
- Prior visa or stay-permit history if already in Indonesia

<h2 id="timeline">Timeline</h2>

Most applications take 3–4 weeks after the sponsor and corporate documents are clean:

- Week 1 — Eligibility review and immigration account preparation
- Week 2 — Application submission and payment instruction
- Week 3 — Approval, biometric or arrival step where applicable
- Week 4 — KITAS issuance and reporting checklist

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>

<strong>Can every shareholder get Investor KITAS?</strong>

Eligibility depends on ownership, role, company documents, and current immigration rules. We check the file before taking the case.

<strong>Can family members apply too?</strong>

Usually after the principal investor permit is clear. We scope dependent permits separately because documents and timing differ.

<strong>Can I work for another company on this permit?</strong>

No. Investor KITAS is tied to the sponsoring company and declared role. Separate work authorization may be needed for employment elsewhere.
