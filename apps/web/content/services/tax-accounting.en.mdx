<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

After setup, Indonesian entities need monthly tax discipline even before revenue is large. We keep books, prepare VAT and income-tax filings, support payroll calculations, and package annual statements for management review.

<h2 id="who-for">Who it's for</h2>

- Newly incorporated PT PMA teams without a local finance hire
- Foreign directors who need clear monthly compliance visibility
- Operating companies that want bookkeeping and tax filing under one accountable team

<h2 id="requirements">Requirements</h2>

- Company NPWP, NIB, deed, and tax account access
- Bank statements and payment records each month
- Sales invoices, vendor invoices, and expense support
- Employee and contractor list for payroll-related tax checks
- Prior filings if we are taking over from another provider

<h2 id="timeline">Timeline</h2>

This is an ongoing monthly service. The first month is used to clean the baseline and agree reporting cadence:

- Day 1–5 — Access handover and prior-period review
- Day 6–15 — Bookkeeping, invoice checks, and payroll inputs
- Day 16–20 — VAT, PPh, and management report preparation
- Year end — Annual financial statement package and tax reconciliation

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>

<strong>Do you replace an internal finance manager?</strong>

No. We handle compliance bookkeeping and filings; larger teams may still need internal finance for approvals, budgets, and treasury.

<strong>Can you fix old filings?</strong>

We can review prior periods and propose corrections. Voluntary amendment strategy depends on the amount, risk, and supporting documents.

<strong>How do we share documents?</strong>

A secure shared folder is enough for v1. We list missing invoices each month so directors can close gaps before filing deadlines.
